# Deliverable Templates (Mode C)

Section-by-section contracts for client-facing artifacts. Every material claim inside these templates still carries its claim label; every number still passes the numeric-consistency gate.

## Executive / Decision Memo (one page)

```markdown
# [Recommendation stated as the title]

## Fact-Base Status
[What was retrieved, what was user-provided, what rests on [A]/[M], what could not be verified.]

## Bottom Line
[Answer in one sentence, confidence, main caveat.]

## Context
[Situation → Complication → Decision required. Three sentences maximum.]

## Recommendation
[Specific action, owner, timing — who does what by when.]

## Rationale
| Argument | Evidence | Label | Confidence | So What |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Alternatives Considered
| Option | Pros | Cons | Why accepted/rejected |
|---|---|---|---|

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Early warning | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Next Actions
| Action | Owner | Deadline | Output / success metric |
|---|---|---|---|

## Confidence / This Flips If / Weakest Link
[H/M/L + reason · 2–3 kill conditions · single assumption most worth attacking.]
```

## Market Research Report

```markdown
# Market Research: [Market]

## Executive Summary        [decision-relevant conclusion, answer-first]
## Fact-Base Status         [retrieval status, source limitations, staleness caveats]
## Market Definition        [boundary, exclusions, geography, time horizon]
## Methodology              [sources, sizing methods, assumptions, limitations]

## Market Size
| Metric | Top-down | Bottom-up | Reconciled range (verbatim everywhere) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
[>30% divergence = investigated finding, never averaged away]

## Segments
| Segment | Need | Size | Growth | Profitability | Competition | Attractiveness | Right to win |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Competitive Landscape    [direct / indirect / substitutes; likely moves with dates]
## Implications             [where to play, how to win, what to avoid]

## Source Table
| Source | Date | Retrieved | Used for | Grade (A–D) | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Assumptions & Sensitivity  [load-bearing assumptions, ranges, 3 weakest tested]
## Confidence / This Flips If / Weakest Link
```

## Due Diligence Workplan

```markdown
# DD Workplan: [Target]

## Deal Question            [decision + transaction context]
## Initial Thesis           [what must be true for the deal to work]

## Must-Believe Assumptions
| Assumption | Why it matters | Evidence needed | Fastest kill test |
|---|---|---|---|

## Workstreams
| Workstream | Key questions | Analyses | Data required | Red flags to watch |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Data Request List        [prioritized, by workstream]

## Red Flag Register
| Red flag | Evidence | Deal impact (price / terms / kill) | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|

## Decision Criteria        [go / no-go / renegotiate thresholds]
```

```markdown
## Fact-Base Status

## Data Sources

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Historical Performance   [key trends; reported vs. adjusted distinguished]

## Assumptions
| Assumption | Base | Downside | Upside | Source/basis + label | Sensitivity |

## Output
| Metric | Downside | Base | Upside |
|---|---|---|---|

## Confidence / This Flips If / Weakest Link
```

## Board Slide Outline (per slide)

```markdown
## Slide [N]: [Action title — states the insight in ≤12 words]

Strategic question:  [what this slide answers]
Governing thought:   [one assertive sentence — the only message of the slide]
Exhibit:             [chart type, axes, data, source — and its so-what]
Evidence:            [material facts with labels and sources]
Speaker note:        [decision implication — the "so what" said aloud]
Risk/caveat:         [what not to overclaim]
```

Slide rules: one governing thought per slide; titles alone must carry the full argument for a skimming reader; no invented metrics — missing data gets a labeled placeholder or a data request; every material number has source and period.
